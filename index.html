How the estimates work

This calculator uses the supplied speed-efficiency model: 1.05 × base MPG at 45–50 mph, 1.12 × at 55 mph, then 1.12 × (1 − 0.018d − 0.0004d²) above 55 mph, where d = speed − 55. Estimated economy has a floor of 10 MPG.

Travel time is distance ÷ speed. Fuel cost is distance ÷ estimated MPG × fuel price. As in the original Python calculation, costs and minutes are rounded before calculating baseline differences. Savings per extra hour is fuel savings divided by additional travel hours.

The supplied formula is an illustrative estimate, not a validated prediction for your vehicle. Actual results depend on traffic, terrain, wind, vehicle, and driving conditions. Trip times assume a constant speed without stops. Choose speeds appropriate to posted limits and conditions.
